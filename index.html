    WHO WE ARE

    CyberTravelling

    travel agency · São Paulo, Brazil

    We are a travel agency for people who want to see where the modern world is made. Our routes go through race circuits, chip markets, car showrooms and launch sites — and through the history, food and landscapes around them.

    Our first product is the China Tech Tour: 28 days that tell one story in growing order — ancient China (the Great Wall, the Forbidden City), industrial China (the Grand Prix, the car museums, BYD), the China of components (Huaqiangbei, Zhongguancun), natural China (Zhangjiajie, Yalong Bay) and space China (Wenchang). It ends next to a rocket launch center.

    THE PARTNERSHIP · JAPAN

    Michio Ayumu

    partner agency

    A Japanese agency running its own Asia route in the same season as ours — they travel in February and March, we travel in March.

    A cruise that boards in one country and lands in another leaves the ship on the wrong side of the map. The operator has to bring it back anyway — so it sells that return cheap. Two agencies filling both ends of the same crossing pay less than one agency filling one end.

    Busan: where the routes split

    Shanghai is to the west, Tokyo to the east, and Busan sits at a similar distance from both — the last port the two groups share. After Busan, our clients go on to Tokyo and theirs go on to China.
